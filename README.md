# Sales & Business Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales and business performance using the Microsoft AdventureWorks sample dataset.

The goal is to explore sales performance across product categories, territories, time periods, and products, and to present the results through interactive dashboards and business insights.

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* SQL Server
* Power BI

## Dataset

The project uses the Microsoft AdventureWorks sample dataset.

The analyzed data covers a period from May 2022 to June 2025.

## Analysis Performed

The project includes:

* Total Sales
* Total Quantity
* Number of Orders
* Number of Customers
* Sales by Category
* Sales by Territory
* Sales Over Time
* Top 10 Products by Sales

## Business Insights

### 1. Sales by Category

Bikes generated the majority of total sales, contributing approximately 86% of overall sales.

### 2. Sales by Territory

Southwest generated the highest total sales among all territories, with approximately $24.18M in sales.

### 3. Sales Over Time

Sales increased across the analyzed period, with several strong monthly peaks in 2024 and early 2025. The dataset covers a partial period in 2022 and 2025, so full-year comparisons should be interpreted with caution.

### 4. Top 10 Products

The top-selling products are mainly Mountain-200 and Road-250 variants, indicating strong sales concentration among these product lines.
